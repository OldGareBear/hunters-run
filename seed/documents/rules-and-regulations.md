# Rules and Regulations (SAMPLE DOCUMENT)

This is fictional sample content for demonstrating the portal. Replace it with the association's real documents.

## 1. Trash and recycling

Trash and recycling containers may be placed at the curb no earlier than 6:00 PM the evening before collection and must be returned out of view from the street by 9:00 PM on collection day. Containers must be stored in the garage or behind a screened enclosure.

## 2. Parking

Residents should park in garages or driveways. Overnight street parking is not permitted between 2:00 AM and 6:00 AM. Guests may park on the street overnight for up to three consecutive nights with a guest pass from the management office. Commercial vehicles, boats and trailers may not be parked in driveways for more than 24 hours.

## 3. Noise

Quiet hours are 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM Sunday through Thursday, and 11:00 PM to 8:00 AM on Friday and Saturday. Construction and landscaping work with power equipment is allowed from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM on weekdays and 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM on Saturdays. No power equipment on Sundays or holidays.

## 4. Pets

Dogs must be leashed in all common areas. Owners must pick up after their pets immediately. No more than three dogs or cats may be kept per household.

## 5. Fines

After a written warning, a first violation may be fined $50, a second violation of the same rule within twelve months $100, and later violations $200 each. Homeowners may request a hearing before the board within 30 days of a fine notice.
