# Architectural Review Guidelines (SAMPLE DOCUMENT)

Fictional sample content for demonstrating the portal.

## When approval is needed

Homeowners must get written approval from the Architectural Review Committee (ARC) before any exterior change, including fences, decks, patios, sheds, exterior paint colors, solar panels, roof replacement with a different material, and removal of trees larger than 6 inches in diameter.

## How to apply

Submit the ARC application form to the management office with a site plan, materials and colors. The ARC reviews complete applications within 30 days. An application with no decision after 45 days is not automatically approved; contact management.

## Fences

Fences may be up to 4 feet tall in side and rear yards and must be black aluminum or white PVC in an open picket style. Front yard fences are not permitted. Pool fences required by code may be up to 5 feet.

## Exterior paint

Exterior colors must be chosen from the approved palette kept by the management office. Repainting in the same existing approved color does not need approval.
