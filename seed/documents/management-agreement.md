# Management Agreement Summary (SAMPLE DOCUMENT)

Fictional sample content, visible to the board and the management company.

## Services

The management company handles collection of assessments, vendor coordination, architectural application intake, resident requests, and preparation of monthly financial statements for the board.

## Response times

The management company will acknowledge resident requests within one business day and resolve or give a status update within five business days. Emergencies affecting common areas are handled 24/7 through the after-hours line.

## Fees

The monthly management fee is $3,950. Additional services, such as attendance at more than one evening meeting per month, are billed at $85 per hour.

## Term

The agreement renews annually on July 1 and either party may end it with 90 days' written notice.
