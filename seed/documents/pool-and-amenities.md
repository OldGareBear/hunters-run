# Pool and Amenities Policy (SAMPLE DOCUMENT)

Fictional sample content for demonstrating the portal.

## Pool season and hours

The pool opens Memorial Day weekend and closes the Sunday after Labor Day. Hours are 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily. There is no lifeguard on duty; swim at your own risk.

## Guests

Each household may bring up to four guests at a time. A resident aged 18 or older must accompany guests at all times. Children under 14 must be accompanied by an adult.

## Clubhouse reservations

Residents in good standing may reserve the clubhouse for private events of up to 50 people. Reservations are made through the management office at least 14 days in advance. A $250 refundable security deposit is required, returned within 10 business days after the event if no damage or extra cleaning is needed. Events must end by 11:00 PM.

## Tennis and pickleball courts

Courts are first come, first served. When others are waiting, play is limited to one hour.
